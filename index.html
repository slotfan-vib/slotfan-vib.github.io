Background

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Gebze Technical University. I received my B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tabriz in 2012, 2014, and 2018, respectively, and later worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Sabancı University.

Current focus

My work centers on vibration-driven mechanics problems that require strong physical modeling, efficient numerical methods, and interpretable computational tools. I am particularly interested in nonlinear structural dynamics, reduced-order models, inverse problems, and mechanics-informed data analysis.

Research focus

Core topics presented in a way that is easy to scan and easy to update later.

Mechanical vibrations

Modeling and analysis of vibration behavior in engineering structures and rotating components.

Nonlinear structural dynamics

High-fidelity formulations for geometrically nonlinear systems, modal interactions, and complex dynamic responses.

Reduced-order and computational modeling

Efficient numerical methods that preserve physical accuracy while reducing model complexity and cost.

Inverse problems and data-enabled mechanics

Signal processing and computational tools for structural identification, health monitoring, and model-informed learning.

Open to motivated students and research collaborators

I welcome inquiries from students and researchers interested in mechanical vibrations, nonlinear dynamics, reduced-order modeling, and related areas in computational mechanics.
