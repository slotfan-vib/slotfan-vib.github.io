I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Gebze Technical University. I earned my B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Mechanical Engineering at University of Tabriz in 2012, 2014, and 2018, respectively. I later joined Sabancı University in Istanbul, Turkey as a postdoctoral researcher.

My primary research interests center on mechanical vibrations, with particular emphasis on both linear and nonlinear systems. I focus on advanced mathematical models and efficient numerical methods for complex structural dynamics. My work also covers reduced-order modeling in nonlinear mechanics, signal processing for inverse problems and structural health monitoring, computational mechanics for high-fidelity simulations, and mechanics-informed data analysis.
