slotfan@gtu.edu.tr

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Gebze Technical University. I received my B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tabriz in 2012, 2014, and 2018, and later worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Sabanci University.

My work focuses on the modeling and analysis of complex structural systems, with emphasis on linear and nonlinear vibrations, reduced-order modeling, inverse problems, structural health monitoring, and high-fidelity computational methods. I also study how data-driven and machine learning approaches can support dynamics and mechanics research.

For current projects, publications, and collaboration opportunities, please use the links above.
